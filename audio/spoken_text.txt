Lifting on 5 Hours of Sleep Kills Your Gains

You’re lifting heavy and eating clean, but your physique hasn’t changed in months. You think you need to train harder—yet you're ignoring the biological sabotage inside your blood.

Every time you cut your sleep to five hours, you pull the emergency brake on your hormone production.

In a JAMA clinical study, men sleeping five hours a night saw their testosterone crash by fifteen percent in just seven days. That is biologically equivalent to aging fifteen years in one week!

Why? Because your body only releases Luteinizing Hormone during deep slow-wave sleep. Cut your sleep, and your brain stops signaling to produce testosterone.

Worse, your cortisol spikes by thirty percent—blocking your androgen receptors and cannibalizing muscle tissue for emergency fuel.
