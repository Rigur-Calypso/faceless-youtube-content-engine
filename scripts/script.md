# How Neural Networks Learn

[SCENE: Glowing digital brain with vibrant neural pulses traveling across synaptic connections]
Have you ever wondered how artificial intelligence actually learns to recognize faces or speak human language?

[SCENE: Complex stream of data numbers transforming into organized geometric patterns]
It doesn't memorize answers. Instead, it starts with complete random guesses and calculates mathematical errors.

[SCENE: Digital scales balancing incoming signals with dynamic weight adjustments]
Every time a prediction is wrong, the network adjusts millions of microscopic connection weights through backpropagation.

[SCENE: Clean smartphone display visualizing real-time voice translation and recognition]
Repeat this optimization process across millions of examples, and intelligent patterns emerge from pure data.
